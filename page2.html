Modern Surfing

Modern surfing has come largely from the evolution of boards. They went from being made of completely wood and lengths exceeding 14 feet to the fiberglass, epoxy, poly, and carbon shortboards of today. These shortboards were originally made for women, but the male shapers at the time started riding them themselves as they liked the style of surfing and the way the board turned. By the 1980s, styles were refined and the modern shortboard came about. It was a 3 fin, 6 foot tall thruster shortboard was the most popular design and still is today.
